Meet the methodology ↗
Where do the prices come from?
Mia calls independent cafés and asks, “How much is a regular flat white?” A price is extracted from the call transcript. The survey focuses on independent cafés. We show the small or regular size, without extras.
How current and complete is the survey?
The dashboard reads the latest available observations when you load it. A fresh dashboard is not proof that every café was called today. Coverage is still growing, and small samples can change quickly. Please confirm prices with the café before ordering.
Can I use the data?
Yes. The data is open under CC BY 4.0. Credit “Flat White Index, flatwhiteindex.com.au” and include the sample size and date you retrieved it. Download the JSON dataset ↗
