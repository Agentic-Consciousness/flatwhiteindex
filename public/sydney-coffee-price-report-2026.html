The current picture

This is an ongoing survey of independent cafés, not a census of Sydney coffee prices. It does not establish a citywide trend or explain why prices vary.

Compare the neighbourhoods

Prices below are averages for the cafés surveyed in each suburb. Select a suburb to explore its cafés on the live map.

A one-observation suburb reflects a single surveyed café. Averages can be tied; alphabetical order breaks ties. Coverage and prices may change.

What we can say

Our survey provides directly reported small flat white prices at the cafés we have reached. It makes those observations easier to compare and available for others to inspect.

What we cannot say yet

The dataset does not represent every café, track all milk or size surcharges, or measure quality. A difference between two suburbs is not evidence of its cause. We also do not yet publish comparable Melbourne data or a validated historical price series.

Use the data responsibly

Attribute Flat White Index, include your retrieval date and sample size, and confirm individual prices with the venue. Read the collection method and limitations.
